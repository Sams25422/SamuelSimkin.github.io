FAQ

Clear boundaries before access.

Does this replace an accountant or bookkeeper?

No. It prepares operational work around a defined workflow. Professional judgment remains with your team.

Do you need production client data immediately?

No. The first build and evaluation use fictional or redacted examples.

What happens when the request is unclear?

The agent stops, shows what is missing, and routes the case for human review.

What happens after the pilot?

You can stop and keep the delivered workflow, expand under a new fixed scope, or add optional monitoring.
